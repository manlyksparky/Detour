## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment:** mostly interchangeable. The system is coherent (one family, one accent, three radii, three shadows, applied without slips on both pages) but coherence is not character. Strip the wordmark and the words Sidequests and Surprise me and this is the stock places-near-me layout. The "Field Notebook" north star never reaches the screen: paper is indistinguishable from white at a glance, the stock Liberty basemap fights the orange pins, and the tour page is the explore page with chips removed, so a tour never reads as a route. The intro is a borrowed loader that says nothing about London or walking.

## Overall Impression
The mechanics are now clean and the drawer is a genuinely good reading screen. The weak spot is the thing the product is named for: the tour is fifteen nearest articles clumped in 500 m, not a walk, and the stops you pick are invisible until you press Plan tour.

## What's Working
- **The floating bar.** Content-sized, nested primary, compacts when the drawer opens; a pill on the map reads as an object.
- **Drawer reading order.** Pills over hero, title, distance, one CTA, prose, "Getting there", attribution. Focus moves in and Escape returns it.
- **Token discipline.** Cards, stops, skeletons and empties share one vocabulary, so the tour page costs nothing to learn.

## Priority Issues
1. **[P0] The tour isn't a walk.** Stops are the 15 nearest with no spacing, so a 1 km radius gives a 500 m clump; "11m from the start" names no start; the title is a borough. Fix: build by target walking time (6 to 10 stops, 45 to 90 minutes), enforce minimum spacing, name by the nearest landmark, draw legs between cards. `/impeccable shape`
2. **[P1] Picks are invisible and volatile.** Add a "Your tour (3)" tray to the sidebar and sheet with remove, and carry the picked stops in the explore URL and the Back link. `/impeccable clarify`
3. **[P1] Non-places lead the explore list.** The place filter already exists for tours; apply it to explore or group areas at the bottom. `/impeccable distill`
4. **[P2] Location asked blind.** Geolocation fires on mount under the intro, so a phone's first sight is a permission prompt over a foreign word. Move it to the Use my location button or after the intro, and label distances "from map centre" when there is no fix. `/impeccable onboard`
5. **[P2] Toolbar overloaded.** Five items in one pill. Drop the zoom buttons (scroll, pinch and keyboard exist), make the search submit secondary. `/impeccable quieter`
